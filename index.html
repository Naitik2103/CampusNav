⚠️ Disclaimer
🏪

Why isn't this on the Play Store?
CampusNav is an academic project developed as part of our university curriculum. Publishing on the Google Play Store requires a one-time developer fee that we, as students, are currently unable to afford. As an alternative, we are distributing the app directly through GitHub — a trusted platform used by millions of developers worldwide.

🔒

Why does Android show a security warning?
Android displays an "Unknown Sources" warning whenever an APK is installed outside the Play Store — this is a standard OS-level precaution, not an indication that our app is harmful. You can safely proceed by tapping Settings → Allow from this source when prompted. The APK is built directly from our open-source code, which you can review at any time on our GitHub repository.

🛡️

Your privacy is fully protected.
CampusNav does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app uses your device's GPS solely to display your current location on the campus map — this data never leaves your device. We are students building a tool to help our campus community, not a commercial entity with any data monetization interest.
